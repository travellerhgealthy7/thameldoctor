Our Services

Comprehensive STD/STI testing, treatment, and men's sexual health services in Kathmandu, Nepal

STD/STI Testing

We provide comprehensive testing for all common sexually transmitted infections, including:

  • HIV Testing (Rapid Test & ELISA)
  • Syphilis (VDRL & TPHA)
  • Gonorrhea (Gram Stain & Culture)
  • Chlamydia (NAAT/PCR Test)
  • Hepatitis B & C
  • Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV) Testing
  • Genital Warts (HPV) Testing

Our testing is conducted in a private, comfortable setting with quick and accurate results. Most test results are available within 24-48 hours.

STD/STI Treatment

We provide effective treatment for all types of sexually transmitted infections, including:

  • Antibiotic therapy for bacterial infections (gonorrhea, chlamydia, syphilis)
  • Antiviral medications for viral infections (herpes, HIV, hepatitis)
  • Topical treatments for genital warts and other skin conditions
  • Follow-up testing to ensure complete recovery
  • Partner treatment recommendations

All treatments are provided with strict confidentiality and personalized care based on your specific condition and medical history.
